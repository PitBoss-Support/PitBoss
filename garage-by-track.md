# Garage by track

One table of the 65 garage cards for an empty draft. **Overall Score** is the mean of the ten absolute base-track scores, and each track has its own score column. A live suggestion still depends on what is on offer and what you have already taken. Higher score is better; rank 1 is best for the column you sort by.

<p class="callout"><strong>These sims may be newer than the in-game PitBoss panel.</strong> Scores use HeatAI’s Oct 24 runs, which have not shipped inside PitBoss yet. The panel still orders base-track garages by average finish, so its order can differ from this Overall Score ranking.</p>

**Methodology:** Each cell is the champion car with one fixed garage card against 4 Legend bots, 500 games per cell. For each of the 10 base BGA Heat tracks, every one of the 65 garage modules was simulated that way by the PitBoss AI. Each track score is 0–100 with one decimal, and higher is better: 60% is normalized average finish (`clamp((5 - avg finish) / 4, 0, 1)`) and 40% is podium rate. Scores are absolute, not rescaled per track, so ceilings differ: USA and France often sit in the high 70s, while Nederland, Mexico, and GB often sit in the low 20s. That is how hard the track is for a one-card car, not a broken AI. Compare cards within a column. A USA 78 and a Nederland 27 are not the same scale. **Overall Score** is the arithmetic mean of those ten absolute track scores, shown to two decimals, so those ceilings flow into Overall. The table opens sorted by Overall Score, highest first, so rank 1…65 follows that order. Click a column header (Overall Score, or a track such as USA or GB) to re-sort the whole table by that column. The chips above the table sort the same way. Tied scores keep the better Oct 24 average-finish rank first (for Overall Score, the better mean of those ranks).

Cards that share a name are different cards. The Card column shows that card's face; the tooltip and screen-reader name use the PitBoss label, such as “4 wheel drive (0, boost)”. Speed uses the PitBoss card glyph: a number, a range such as 1-5, or + / ++ / +++ when the printed speed is 0. Details shows the HUD symbols.

Card faces and those symbols are the same Board Game Arena Heat art the PitBoss extension uses.

**Freshness:** 500 sims per module per track · ranking data generated 2026-10-24T21:23:28Z (2026-10-24 CT) · source HeatAI `data/garage_draft/track_module_one_card_rankings.json` @ `e03ccea` · exported from HeatAI master @ `b0639ea`

These scores update only when HeatAI regenerates this export.

Non-base tracks are not listed. They are scored live in the panel, and there is no static table for them.
