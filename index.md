# PitBoss

<p class="badges">
  <span class="badge">Beta</span>
  <span class="badge badge-ver">v0.9.0</span>
  <span class="badge badge-free">Free during the beta</span>
</p>

PitBoss is an unofficial Chrome extension for *Heat: Pedal to the Metal* on [Board Game Arena](https://boardgamearena.com). It adds a movable panel on your Heat table, plus a few on-board extras, so you can see draft rankings, engine and boost info, play-speed odds, a HeatAI move suggestion, deck and discard tracking, and opponent summaries without leaving the game.

It runs on Chrome and Brave on the desktop (Manifest V3). It only runs on `boardgamearena.com`, and the panel only appears on a Heat game table — not in the lobby.

**PitBoss is free during the beta.**
