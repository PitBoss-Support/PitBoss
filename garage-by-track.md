# Garage by track

One table of the 65 garage cards for an empty draft. Choose **Avg** or a base track. A live suggestion still depends on what is on offer and what you have already taken. Higher score is better; rank 1 is best for the filter you have selected.

<p class="callout"><strong>These sims may be newer than the in-game PitBoss panel.</strong> Scores use HeatAI’s Oct 24 runs, which have not shipped inside PitBoss yet. The panel still orders base-track garages by average finish, so its order can differ from this Score ranking.</p>

**Methodology:** For each of the 10 base BGA Heat tracks, every one of the 65 garage modules was raced alone in 500 simulated 4-car races by the PitBoss AI. **Score** is 0–100 with one decimal, and higher is better. It uses a 6-player scale: 60% normalized average finish (`clamp((6 - avg finish) / 5, 0, 1)`) and 40% podium rate. **Avg** is the arithmetic mean of those ten track scores, shown to two decimals. Choosing a chip re-ranks the 65 cards by the score for that filter. Tied scores keep the better Oct 24 average-finish rank first (for Avg, the better mean of those ranks).

Cards that share a name are different cards. The Card column shows that card's face; the tooltip and screen-reader name use the PitBoss label, such as “4 wheel drive (0, boost)”. Speed uses the PitBoss card glyph: a number, a range such as 1-5, or + / ++ / +++ when the printed speed is 0. Details shows the HUD symbols.

Card faces and those symbols are the same Board Game Arena Heat art the PitBoss extension uses.

**Freshness:** 500 sims per module per track · ranking data generated 2026-10-24T21:23:28Z (2026-10-24 CT) · source HeatAI `data/garage_draft/track_module_one_card_rankings.json` @ `e03ccea` · exported from HeatAI master @ `b0639ea`

These scores update only when HeatAI regenerates this export.

Non-base tracks are not listed. They are scored live in the panel, and there is no static table for them.

Advisory only. PitBoss is not affiliated with or endorsed by Days of Wonder or Board Game Arena. A simulation is not a guarantee of how a module will finish in your game.

Per-track rows, including Score, are in [garage-by-track.csv]({{ '/assets/data/garage-by-track.csv' | relative_url }}) (500 sims each). Card definitions are in [garage-modules.csv]({{ '/assets/data/garage-modules.csv' | relative_url }}).
