# Known limitations

PitBoss is a **v0.9.0 beta**. It runs on Chrome and Brave on the desktop. Firefox is not supported yet.

- **Card tracking can drift.** Reload first. If it stays wrong, click **Reset**, which clears saved tracking for that table and reloads. Playing from another device or a missed update is a usual cause.
- **Unrecognised BGA formats pause advice.** The `BGA format not recognized` banner means advice waits until the format is understood, and card tracking may be incomplete.
- **The Narrow Streets expansion is not supported yet.**
- **The AI handles at most 8 opponents.**
- **Lookahead search** is local and slower. In the current friends-beta build it is on until you turn it off.
- **Garage-by-track tables on this site may be newer than the in-game panel** until the Oct 24 rankings ship inside PitBoss.
- **There is no in-panel settings screen.** Settings opens Options. Changes apply immediately.
- **Auto-show HUD on Game Start** is saved but has no effect yet.

Landing estimates on opponents use public info only.

If you hit something that is not on this list, [report it]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }}).
